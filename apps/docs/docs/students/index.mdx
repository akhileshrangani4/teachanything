Your instructor has set up an AI assistant that has read your course materials — the readings, slides, and notes for your class. You can ask it questions any time and get answers based on those materials.

Think of it as a study buddy who has done all the reading and can point you to exactly where something is explained.

## How to open it

Depending on your course, you'll reach the assistant one of two ways:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A link" icon="link">
    Your instructor shares a web link. Click it and start chatting — no login
    needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A chat button" icon="message-circle">
    On your course website, look for a chat button in the corner of the page.
    Click it to open the assistant.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Asking a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Write it just like you'd text a classmate. Full sentences work best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    The assistant answers using your course materials. If your instructor
    enabled it, you'll also see which materials the answer came from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the conversation going">
    Ask follow-up questions. The assistant remembers what you were just talking
    about.
  </Step>
</Steps>

:::tip[Some assistants can hear you]
If your instructor enabled voice, you can tap the microphone and speak your question instead of typing. Your browser will ask permission to use your mic the first time.
:::

## What to expect

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="It sticks to your course" icon="book">
    It answers from your class materials, not the whole internet, so answers
    stay relevant to what you're studying.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It shows its sources" icon="quote">
    When sources are turned on, you can see and double-check the original
    material.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It won't always know" icon="help-circle">
    If something isn't in the course materials, it will say so rather than
    guess.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It's a study aid" icon="graduation-cap">
    It's here to help you learn — not to do graded work for you. Check your
    course's rules on using AI.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
