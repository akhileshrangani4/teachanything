Teach Anything is free for instructors, and the cost of running the AI models is shared by the whole community. To keep it fair, every assistant and every instructor has a daily message limit. One very busy class can't use up what everyone else needs.

## How the limits work

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="150 per assistant" icon="bot">
    Each assistant can answer up to 150 student questions a day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="300 per instructor" icon="user">
    All of your assistants together can answer up to 300 questions a day.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

- **What counts:** each question a student sends counts as one message. The assistant's answer does not count separately, so a conversation with 10 questions counts as 10.
- **Your own tests count too.** Questions you ask while trying out your assistant in the dashboard use the same daily allowance.
- **When it resets:** at midnight US Eastern time (New York) every day.
- **Both limits apply.** An assistant stops at 150 even if your 300 total isn't used up. Once your total reaches 300, all of your assistants stop for the day.

:::tip[What 150 looks like]
Most assistants use far less: a typical assistant gets about 5 questions a day. 150 is roughly 30 students asking 5 questions each.
:::

## See how much you've used

Your **Chatbots** page shows how many questions your assistants have answered today:

- a total across all of your assistants, against your daily limit;
- a count on each assistant, against its own limit.

The bar turns amber when an assistant is close to its limit and red once the limit is reached.

## What students see

<Steps>
  <Step title="When the limit is reached">
    Students see: _"This chatbot has reached its daily message limit. Please try
    again tomorrow."_ The question they typed stays in the box, so they can send
    it again after the reset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When an assistant is paused">
    A platform administrator can pause an assistant, for example while sorting
    out unusually heavy use. Students then see: _"This chatbot is currently
    unavailable."_
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everyone else's assistants keep working normally in both cases.

## Planning a large class

If you're planning an in-class activity with a large group, a single assistant may reach its limit partway through.

- **Tell us ahead of time.** Email [admin@teachanything.ai](mailto:admin@teachanything.ai) before the session, and we can look at raising the limit for that assistant.
- **Consider contributing.** If your department or school can contribute to the platform's computing costs, we can raise your limits for the term.
- **Spread the questions out.** Asking students to use the assistant before or after class, not all at once, keeps everyone under the limit.

:::note[Why not unlimited?]
Every answer costs a small amount to generate. Teach Anything runs on a small shared budget, and one class of a few hundred students asking questions at once can use up a week's worth in under an hour. The limits keep the platform available to every instructor.
:::
