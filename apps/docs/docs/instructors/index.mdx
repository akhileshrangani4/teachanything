This guide walks you through building an AI teaching assistant for your course, from your first login to sharing it with students. No technical background needed.

## The big picture

Think of your assistant as a tutor who has read all of your course materials and nothing else. When a student asks a question, it looks through your files, finds the relevant parts, and writes an answer that points back to the exact source. It won't make things up from the open internet, and it will tell students where each answer came from.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request an instructor account">
    Accounts are approved by an administrator to keep the platform limited to
    verified university professors. Please sign up with your university email
    and wait for approval. Reach out to
    [admin@teachanything.ai](mailto:admin@teachanything.ai) if your university
    or country's domain is rejected. We will have to verify your status and
    possibly manually add your domain name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a chatbot">
    Give your assistant a name and a short description of how it should behave.
    See [Create a chatbot](/instructors/create-a-chatbot).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your materials">
    [Upload files](/instructors/upload-materials) or [point it at a
    website](/instructors/add-website-content). The assistant reads and
    remembers everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it with students">
    [Share the permalink](/instructors/share-and-embed) or [embed the
    assistant](/instructors/share-and-embed) directly in your course site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting an account

Instructor sign-ups go through a short approval step so only real instructors get access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    [Create an account](https://teachanything.ai/register) with your name, email
    address, and a password. Some deployments only let certain email domains
    (like your school's) register, so use your institutional email if you have
    one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    An administrator reviews new accounts. You'll be able to log in once your
    account is approved. This is usually quick, but it isn't instant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Once approved, [sign in](https://teachanything.ai/login) and you'll land on
    your [dashboard](https://teachanything.ai/dashboard), where all of your
    assistants live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

:::note[Password requirements]
Choose a strong password. The sign-up form will tell you if your password is too weak and what to fix.
:::

## What you'll manage

Once you're in, everything revolves around your [dashboard](https://teachanything.ai/dashboard):

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your chatbots" icon="bot">
    Each course (or topic) can have its own assistant. Create as many as you
    need.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Materials" icon="folder">
    The files and web pages each assistant has learned from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conversations" icon="messages-square">
    An anonymized and searchable record of what students asked and how the
    assistant answered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Account settings" icon="settings">
    Your profile, privacy controls, and account deletion.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
