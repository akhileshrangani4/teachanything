Your first step is to create the assistant itself. This is quick — you can always change everything later. From your [dashboard](https://teachanything.ai/dashboard), click **New chatbot** and fill out the form.

## Name and description

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name">
    What your assistant is called, e.g. "BIO 101 Study Helper." Students see
    this name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Description (optional)">
    A short note about what the assistant covers. This is mostly for you, to
    tell your assistants apart on the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Instructions (the assistant's personality)

The **instructions** field is the most important setting. It tells the assistant how to behave — its tone, what it should focus on, and any rules you want it to follow. Write it in plain English, as if you were briefing a teaching assistant on their first day.

:::tip[A good starting point]

> You are a friendly teaching assistant for an introductory biology course. Answer using only the uploaded course materials. Explain concepts simply, use examples, and encourage students to think through problems rather than just giving away answers. If something isn't covered in the materials, say so and suggest the student ask the instructor.
> :::

You don't have to get this perfect. Start simple, watch how it answers, and adjust.

## How it answers

A few settings control the style of the answers:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI model" icon="cpu">
    The underlying AI that writes the answers. Several are available; the
    default is a strong general-purpose choice. See [Customize your
    assistant](/instructors/customize#choosing-a-model).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creativity" icon="sparkles">
    Lower values keep answers focused and predictable — best for factual course
    material. Higher values make answers more varied.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Answer length" icon="ruler">
    A cap on how long each answer can get. Longer allows more detail; shorter
    keeps things concise.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Show sources" icon="quote">
    When on, answers list which of your files they drew from, so students can
    check the original.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Save it

Click **Create**, and your assistant appears on your dashboard. It doesn't know anything yet — the next step is to feed it your course materials.
